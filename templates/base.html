6 Operational Steps of the System
End-to-End Pipeline
Step 01 Stream Ingestion & Normalization

Captures CCTV video or RTSP IP camera streams at 30 FPS, applying frame stabilization, letterbox padding, and dimensional normalization to 640 × 480 for edge throughput.

Step 02 YOLOv8 Multi-Class Entity Detection

Single-shot anchor-free CNN localizes locomotives, track maintenance wagons, and pedestrians with tight bounding boxes, class probability distributions, and high confidence.

Step 03 Polygonal ROI Track Geofencing

Shapely computational geometry checks the foot coordinates and centroid of detected pedestrians against dynamic 4-point track polygons, separating active tracks from safe platforms.

Step 04 PPE & Biometric Whitelisting

Applies HSV color masks to analyze high-vis fluorescent orange vests (≥7%) and yellow hardhats (≥3.5%), coupled with FaceNet biometric embedding matching to whitelist authorized crew.

Step 05 Kinematic Speed & TTC Collision Engine

Calculates train velocity (km/h) via optical track displacement and computes predictive Time-to-Collision (TTC ≤ 4.0s). Escalates through 4 alert tiers and triggers warning sirens.

Step 06 Cryptographic SHA-256 Ledger Logging

Every intrusion incident is hashed in sequence with the previous block hash ($H_i = \text{SHA256}(H_{i-1} \parallel \text{Payload})$) and stored in SQLite for tamper-evident forensic auditing.
